Delete Your Account

You can permanently delete your StreetEats account and all associated data using either method below.

⚠ This action is permanent and cannot be undone
  • Your profile and personal information
  • Any stalls you've registered as a vendor
  • All reviews you've posted
Option 1 — Delete in the app
  1. Open the StreetEats app on your phone
  2. Tap the account icon (top right)
  3. Scroll down and tap Delete account
  4. Confirm in the popup — your account will be deleted immediately
or
Option 2 — Request via email

Send an email to quixentapps@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my StreetEats account" and include your registered phone number. We will process your request within 7 business days.
