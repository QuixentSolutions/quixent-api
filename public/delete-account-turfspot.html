Delete Your Account

You can permanently delete your TurfSpot account and all associated data using either method below.

⚠ This action is permanent and cannot be undone
  • Your profile and personal information
  • Any turfs you've listed as an owner
  • Your booking history
  • All reviews you've posted
Option 1 — Delete in the app
  1. Open the TurfSpot app on your phone
  2. Go to the Profile tab
  3. Scroll down and tap Delete account
  4. Confirm in the popup — your account will be deleted immediately
or
Option 2 — Request via email

Send an email to quixentapps@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my TurfSpot account" and include your registered phone number. We will process your request within 7 business days.
